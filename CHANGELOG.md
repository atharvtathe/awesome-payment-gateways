# Changelog

Fee changes, country changes and new providers, newest first. Each entry was checked against the provider's own pages.

## November 2026

**Changed**

- The list is now global instead of country by country. Removed the "Can I Sell From My Country" table and the country guides. Each provider's "Sell from" column links its own country list. Earlier versions are in the git history.
- Local gateways are now one Regional Gateways table instead of a section per region.

**New**

- Payment gateways: Airwallex (2.8% + 30¢ US cards, sellers in 69 countries and territories), Square (online 3.3% + 30¢ on the Free plan, 8 countries) and Authorize.net (2.9% + 30¢ + $25/mo).
- Merchant of record platforms: 2Checkout (Verifone), with payout details, and PayPro Global (pricing on request).
- Airwallex added to Receive Your Payouts.
- Payment Methods Buyers Can Use: cards, PayPal, wallets and local methods for Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Creem and Dodo Payments.
- Disputes and Refunds: dispute and refund fees for Stripe, Square, Paddle, Polar, Kelviq, Creem and Dodo Payments.
- Cleverbridge (merchant of record, pricing on request), Revolut Business (gateway, 1% + £0.20 on UK consumer cards) and Grey (USD, GBP and EUR accounts).
- US bank accounts for a US company: Mercury and Relay.
- What You Can Sell: AI products, services, courses, crypto and physical goods under each provider's policy, for seven merchant of record platforms and Stripe.
- Reviews and Payout Holds: account review time, payout timing, holds and reserves for seven providers.
- Paddle's payouts (wire or Payoneer, $100 minimum, monthly) and Dodo Payments' payout minimum and schedule.
- Comparison tables now also cover Fungies, Freemius, Kelviq and Stripe where they publish the data, and Lemon Squeezy's $15 dispute fee.
- Corrected Mercado Pago's credit card fee (up to 4.98%) and Fungies' payouts (no minimum, no payout fee).
- What You Can Sell and Reviews and Payout Holds moved up, next to the provider tables.
- Lemon Squeezy's surcharges (+1.5% international, +1.5% PayPal, +0.5% subscriptions) and payout fees.

- Kelviq, a merchant of record for SaaS and AI: 3.5% + 40¢ (2.9% + 40¢ until $5,000 in sales), sellers in 120 countries, no payout fee. Added to the country table and guides (since removed).
- "No company needed?" column for merchant of record platforms. Paddle, Dodo Payments, Gumroad and Freemius state on their own pages that individuals can sell without a registered company.
- Payout details for each MoR: methods, minimums and schedule.
- Country guides (since removed) for India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Indonesia and Brazil.
- Providers: PayU, Instamojo (India), aamarPay (Bangladesh), Telr (UAE), Braintree (global).
- Receive Your Payouts section: Wise and Payoneer.
- Seller countries for Fungies (55) and Freemius (190+).

**Worth knowing**

- Stripe lists India and Indonesia as Preview (invite only), and sends sign-ups in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa and Côte d'Ivoire to Paystack.
- Dodo Payments doesn't onboard new sellers in Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Kenya or Egypt.
- Polar doesn't pay out to Brazil or Nepal. Lemon Squeezy pays out to both only through PayPal.
- Lemon Squeezy's site now shows a "Lemon Squeezy + Stripe Managed Payments" update.

**First release**

- Country table for 18 countries, 10 merchant of record platforms, global and local gateways, and US company formation services.
