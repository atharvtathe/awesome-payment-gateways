# Changelog

Fee changes, country changes and new providers, newest first. Each entry was checked against the provider's own pages.

## November 2026

**New**

- "No company needed?" column for merchant of record platforms. Paddle, Dodo Payments, Gumroad and Freemius state on their own pages that individuals can sell without a registered company.
- Payout details for each MoR: methods, minimums and schedule.
- Country guides for [India](countries/india.md), [Pakistan](countries/pakistan.md), [Bangladesh](countries/bangladesh.md), [Nigeria](countries/nigeria.md), [Indonesia](countries/indonesia.md) and [Brazil](countries/brazil.md).
- Providers: PayU, Instamojo (India), aamarPay (Bangladesh), Telr (UAE), Braintree (global).
- Receive Your Payouts section: Wise and Payoneer.
- Seller countries for Fungies (55) and Freemius (190+).

**Worth knowing**

- Stripe lists India and Indonesia as Preview (invite only), and sends sign-ups in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa and Côte d'Ivoire to Paystack.
- Dodo Payments doesn't onboard new sellers in Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Kenya or Egypt.
- Polar doesn't pay out to Brazil or Nepal. Lemon Squeezy pays out to both only through PayPal.
- Lemon Squeezy's site now shows a "Lemon Squeezy + Stripe Managed Payments" update.

**First release**

- Country table for 18 countries, 10 merchant of record platforms, global and local gateways, and US company formation services.
