# Getting Paid From Bangladesh

[← Back to Awesome Payment Gateways](../README.md)

**Quick answer:** Stripe isn't available in Bangladesh. To sell to customers worldwide, use a merchant of record (MoR) that pays out to Bangladesh: Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar or Kelviq. To sell to customers in Bangladesh (cards, bKash, Nagad, Rocket), use SSLCommerz or aamarPay.

_Last checked: November 2026. Confirm on each provider's page before you sign up._

## Stripe

Bangladesh isn't on [Stripe's country list](https://stripe.com/global). The common workaround is to [open a US company](../README.md#no-option-in-your-country-open-a-us-company), which adds US tax filings.

## Things to know

- **Local gateways expect business documents.** aamarPay's sign-up asks for a trade license, TIN, NID and company papers ([pricing](https://aamarpay.com/pricing)). MoRs are usually the easier start for a solo founder.
- **aamarPay doesn't accept virtual or prepaid cards** for international payments, only cards issued by a bank.
