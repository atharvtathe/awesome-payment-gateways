# Getting Paid From Nigeria

[← Back to Awesome Payment Gateways](../README.md)

**Quick answer:** Stripe sends Nigerian sign-ups to Paystack, which it owns. Paystack works well for Nigerian customers and also takes international cards. To sell to customers worldwide with sales tax handled for you, use a merchant of record (MoR) that pays out to Nigeria: Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar or Creem.

_Last checked: November 2026. Confirm on each provider's page before you sign up._

## Stripe

[Stripe's country page](https://stripe.com/global) lists Nigeria under its **extended network** and links to Paystack instead of Stripe's own sign-up.

## Selling to customers in Nigeria (and some abroad)

| Provider                                                                | Local payments                | International cards    | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------ |

| [Flutterwave](https://flutterwave.com/us/pricing)                       | See pricing page              | See pricing page       | –                        |

## Selling to customers worldwide (MoRs)

| Provider                                                                                        | Pays out to Nigeria | Fee                  | Notes                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/pricing)                                                        | Yes:                | 5% + 50¢             | Business check skipped for individuals and sole traders |
| [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing)                                           | Yes: Bank payouts   | 5% + 50¢             | $50 minimum payout                                      |
| [Polar](https://polar.sh/resources/pricing)                                                     | Yes:                | 5% + 50¢ (free plan) | Payouts through Stripe Connect Express                  |
| [Creem](https://www.creem.io/pricing)                                                           | Yes:                | 3.9% + 40¢           | Payout fee: 7 USD or 1%, whichever is higher            |
| [Dodo Payments](https://docs.dodopayments.com/miscellaneous/accepted-countries-and-territories) | No                  | –                    | Kept only for existing merchants, not new sign-ups      |
| [Fungies](https://help.fungies.io/getting-paid/global-availability)                             | No                  | –                    | Not on its payout list                                  |

## Things to know

- **Paystack vs an MoR:** Paystack is cheaper per sale, but you handle VAT and sales tax for foreign customers. An MoR costs more and handles that for you.
- **Paystack's ₦2,000 cap** applies only to local transactions. Its published pages don't mention a cap on international card fees.
