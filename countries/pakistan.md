# Getting Paid From Pakistan

[← Back to Awesome Payment Gateways](../README.md)

**Quick answer:** Stripe isn't available in Pakistan. To sell to customers worldwide, use a merchant of record (MoR) that pays out to Pakistan: Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar or Kelviq. To sell to customers in Pakistan, use a local gateway such as Safepay.

_Last checked: November 2026. Confirm on each provider's page before you sign up._

## Stripe

Pakistan isn't on [Stripe's country list](https://stripe.com/global). The common workaround is to [open a US company](../README.md#no-option-in-your-country-open-a-us-company) and use Stripe through it, which adds US tax filings.

## Things to know

- **Check payouts before you build.** An MoR approving your account and paying out to your country are separate steps. Read each provider's payout country list for Pakistan first.
- **Dodo Payments and Fungies** don't currently onboard sellers in Pakistan, even though both sell to customers worldwide.
