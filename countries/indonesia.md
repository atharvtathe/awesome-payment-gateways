# Getting Paid From Indonesia

[← Back to Awesome Payment Gateways](../README.md)

**Quick answer:** Stripe is invite-only in Indonesia. To sell to customers worldwide, every major merchant of record (MoR) on this list pays out to Indonesia. To sell to Indonesian customers, use Xendit.

_Last checked: November 2026. Confirm on each provider's page before you sign up._

## Stripe

[Stripe's country page](https://stripe.com/global) lists Indonesia as **Preview**, and sign-ups go to sales.

## Selling to customers worldwide (MoRs)

| Provider                                                            | Pays out to Indonesia | Fee                  | Notes                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/pricing)                            | Yes:                  | 5% + 50¢             | Business check skipped for individuals and sole traders |
| [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing)               | Yes: Bank payouts     | 5% + 50¢             | $50 minimum payout                                      |
| [Polar](https://polar.sh/resources/pricing)                         | Yes:                  | 5% + 50¢ (free plan) | Payouts through Stripe Connect Express                  |
| [Creem](https://www.creem.io/pricing)                               | Yes:                  | 3.9% + 40¢           | Payout fee: 7 USD or 1%, whichever is higher            |
| [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/pricing)                   | Yes:                  | 4% + 40¢             | "Individual" sign-up path                               |
| [Fungies](https://help.fungies.io/getting-paid/global-availability) | Yes:                  | 5% + 50¢             | Daily payouts by default                                |

## Selling to customers in Indonesia (local gateway)

| Provider                                                                                                 | Cards                               | Other costs  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------ |
| [Xendit](https://help.xendit.co/hc/en-us/articles/360039086452-What-are-the-pricing-for-Xendit-products) | 2.9% + IDR 2,000 (Visa, Mastercard) | Excludes VAT |

## Things to know

- **Indonesia has the widest MoR choice** of the countries in this list. Pick on fees and features, not availability.
- **Xendit** charges JCB and Amex at the bank's fee plus IDR 2,000, not the standard card rate ([pricing](https://help.xendit.co/hc/en-us/articles/360039086452-What-are-the-pricing-for-Xendit-products)).
